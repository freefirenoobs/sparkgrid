Slide 2 / 7 • The Remote Sensing Dilemma 🔥 SPARKGRID • SENSOR PHYSICS

Why Raw MODIS (1km) & VIIRS (375m) Feeds Clash

Spatial Granularity, Point-Spread Broadening & Orbital Overpass Skew

🟠 MODIS (Terra & Aqua Satellites): Scans at 1000m × 1000m nadir resolution (1.00 km² footprint area) using 3.96 µm Channels 21/22. Outputs continuous 0–100% confidence at ~10:30 AM and ~1:30 PM local overpasses.
🔴 VIIRS (Suomi-NPP, NOAA-20 & NOAA-21): Scans at 375m × 375m I-Band I4/I5 resolution (0.14 km² footprint area — 7.1× tighter!). Outputs categorical low / nominal / high confidence with minimal scan-edge distortion.
⚠️ The Duplication Hazard: A single 1 km² wildfire triggers 1 large MODIS pixel + up to 7 overlapping VIIRS pixels. Summing raw feeds overestimates burn area and confuses aerial tanker dispatchers.

Slide 3 / 7 • Algorithmic Formulation 🔥 SPARKGRID • HARMONIZATION ENGINE

The 8-Stage Spatiotemporal Harmonization Pipeline

Spherical Haversine DBSCAN + FRP-Weighted Centroids + Bayesian Fusion

1️⃣ Unified Confidence Mapping: Normalizes MODIS integer confidence c = conf / 100 and VIIRS categorical tiers (low=0.30, nominal=0.70, high=0.95) into a shared Bayesian space [0.0, 1.0].
2️⃣ Great-Circle Haversine DBSCAN: Groups detections on the spherical Earth using ε = 1.5 km (0.0002354 rad) and orbital time window |Δt| ≤ 120 minutes.
3️⃣ FRP-Weighted Centroids & Bayesian Reinforcement: Anchors cluster coordinates to peak Fire Radiative Power Center = ∑(x_i · FRP_i) / ∑FRP_i and fuses multi-sensor probability via C_fused = 1 − ∏(1 − c_i).
